Promoting Integrity, Transparency and Accountability

Welcome to the official Integrity and Governance Division Management System of Sawit Kinabalu. We are committed to upholding the highest standards of ethical conduct, transparency, and accountability across all operations. Together, we build a culture of trust and compliance.

Our Mission

To foster a culture of integrity, ethical governance, and accountability by implementing robust frameworks, policies, and programmes that prevent misconduct and promote transparency across all levels of the organisation.

Our Vision

To be a leading centre of excellence in organisational integrity and governance, recognised for cultivating an environment where ethical conduct is the standard and where every stakeholder upholds the principles of fairness and responsibility.
